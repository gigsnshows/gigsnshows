Privacy

Last updated 6 October 2026

gigsnshows lists live music, theatre, sports and stand-up from ticketing sites and venues. We don't sell tickets: when you book, you do it on the seller's own site, and their privacy policy applies there.

If you just browse

Nothing about you is kept on our servers. Your city, interests, Wanna Go list and the shows you've opened, saved or searched for (which shape For you) are stored in your browser only. “Forget what I've browsed” under Your interests clears the history, and clearing your browser's site data clears everything.

Analytics

Only if you tap OK on the cookie notice, we use Google Analytics to see which pages and features people use: for example which row a show was opened from, which ticket site was chosen, and words typed into search. It's never used for advertising. Until you say OK, Google receives only cookie-free signals that don't identify your device. Analytics data is deleted after 14 months.

Booking by voice

When you use the mic, your browser's own speech service (Google's in Chrome, Apple's in Safari) turns what you say into text. We don't record or keep your voice or what you said; analytics only notes whether a show was found.

If you log in

Logging in is optional. If you do, we keep:

  • Your mobile number, to send you a one-time code and recognise you. This is handled by Google's Firebase Authentication, which also runs a reCAPTCHA check to keep out bots.
  • Your Wanna Go list, interests and For you history, so they follow you to every device you log in on. These are stored in Google Cloud Firestore and can be read only by you.

We use these only to log you in and keep your picks. We don't sell or share them, and we don't send marketing messages.

Plans with friends (“Who's in?”)

If you start a plan or answer one, we keep the plan (the show, its date and venue), the first name you typed and your answer (Going, Maybe or Can't). Anyone who has the plan's link can see the names and answers on it; nobody can browse plans without a link. You don't need to log in: your browser is given an anonymous identifier so your answer stays yours. This is stored in Google Cloud Firestore.

You can change or remove your answer, or delete a plan you started, from the plan itself at any time. Deleting your account also deletes the plans you started and your answers on the plans your account is linked to.

Shared Wanna Go lists

If you create or join a shared list, we keep the list's name, the first name you typed and the shows on your Wanna Go list, so the group can see what everyone wants to go to. Anyone who has the list's link can see these names and picks; nobody can browse lists without a link. You don't need to log in: your browser is given an anonymous identifier so your entry stays yours. This is stored in Google Cloud Firestore. While you're a member, changes to your Wanna Go list update your entry.

You can leave a list at any time, which removes your name and picks from it, and the person who made a list can delete it. Deleting your account also deletes the lists you made and your entries in lists you joined.

Deleting your data

Tap the account icon at the top, then Delete my account. That removes your number and everything saved to your account straight away. You can also write to us to ask what we hold about you, to correct it or to delete it, as India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 provides.

Contact

Questions or complaints about your data: use our contact form. We'll reply by email within 30 days. Messages sent through the form are delivered to our inbox by Web3Forms, and we use your name and email only to reply.
