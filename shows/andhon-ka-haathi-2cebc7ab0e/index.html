ANDHON KA HAATHI

Sun 1 Nov, 7 pm · Padatik Little Theatre, Kolkata · from ₹200

Stand-up comedyHindi

This satirical play adapts the well-known parable of ‘the blind men and an elephant’ into a sharp edged, comic depiction of contemporary politics, societal apathy and inaction. The elephant becomes a metaphor for the absence of unity in tackling problematic and unresolved issues.
