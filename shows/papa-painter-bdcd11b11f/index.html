A father, a son, and the distance that quietly grows between them. KCC presents Papa Painter, a theatrical work set in 1990s India, where migration, memory, and change shape an intimate story of family and identity. At its heart is a son’s journey from knowing someone simply as ‘Papa’ to discovering the person behind the word.
