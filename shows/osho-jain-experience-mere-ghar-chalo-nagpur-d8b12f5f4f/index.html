Osho Jain Experience – Mere Ghar Chalo - Nagpur

Sat 6 Feb, 7 pm · Vasantrao Deshpande Hall, Nagpur

TheatreMusicals

All shows at Vasantrao Deshpande Hall

Come home with Osho Jain. Mere Ghar Chalo is an intimate musical experience where Osho invites the audience into his world - taking them back to his childhood, the places he grew up, the memories that shaped him, and the stories that found their way into his music. Through soulful songs, personal anecdotes, and moments of nostalgia, Osho creates an evening that feels less like a concert and more like spending time…
