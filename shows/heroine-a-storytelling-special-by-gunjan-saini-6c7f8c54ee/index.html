Heroine - A Storytelling Special by Gunjan Saini

We dream fearlessly, without logic, limits and permission.Then the world steps up with the labels, expectations and stories about who we should be.Heroine is a storytelling experience about growing up, losing yourself in other people definitions, and finding your way back.
