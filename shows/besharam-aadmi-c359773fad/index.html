Besharam Aadmi

Sun 13 Dec · Kolkata Centre for Creativity, Kolkata

KCC presents Besharam Aadmi, performed by Amol Parashar and written and directed by Vijay Ashok Sharma. It begins with a seemingly ordinary moment: a young man hesitates to handle his wife’s lingerie in front of his parents.
