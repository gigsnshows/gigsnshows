Mere Krishn – A Live Theatrical Experience of Shree Krishn's Life

Fri 25 Dec · Prestige Centre for Performing Arts, Bengaluru

TheatreStorytelling & poetryDance

Mere Krishn is an immersive theatrical experience that journeys through the divine, human & philosophical dimensions of Shree Krishn's life. The Play unfolds across 21 vivid scenes from his childhood in vrindavan to Mahabharat to his final moments in dwarka.
