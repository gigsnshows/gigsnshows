Miniature Cheriyal Mask

Sat 10 Oct, 3 pm · Hitchki, Powai, Mumbai · 2 dates

TheatreStorytelling & poetry

Discover the rich storytelling tradition of Cheriyal art by creating your very own miniature Cheriyal mask. Inspired by the traditional folk art of Telangana, Cheriyal art is known for its vibrant colours, expressive characters, and distinctive visual style. Learn about the artistic elements behind this traditional Indian art form as you paint and decorate a miniature mask with bold colours and intricate details…
