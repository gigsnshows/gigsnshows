ISKATE by Roseate

Tue 27 Oct, 8 pm · ISKATE by Roseate, Delhi · from ₹1,000

Sports

India’s largest indoor ice skating rink, ISKATE by Roseate is located on the 6th floor of Ambience Mall, Gurugram. Spread over a sprawling 15,000 sq. ft. it gives one the opportunity to experience skating on real ice. With a capacity of up to 150 skaters at any given point of time, the amenities at ISKATE by Roseate include Roasted By Roseate, a luxe co-working space, ISKATE school, and a snow room.
