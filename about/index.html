About gigsnshows

gigsnshows brings live music, stand-up, theatre and sports from across India into one place, picked around your taste. 2,006 shows across 65 cities as of 27 October 2026.

What it does

Instead of checking each ticketing site one by one, you see what's on in your city on one page: tonight, this weekend or on a date you pick, with filters for music, stand-up, theatre and sports. You can save shows, plan a night out with friends and share a plan on WhatsApp.

Where the listings come from

The listings are gathered from the public pages of BookMyShow, District, Skillbox, thumpN, NCPA, Prithvi Theatre, The Piano Man and NMACC. Each show links back to the seller, because that is where tickets are bought. When the same show appears on more than one site, it is shown once with a link for each.

Cities

Mumbai, Bengaluru, Delhi, Pune, Hyderabad, Kolkata, Ahmedabad, Goa, Chennai, Jaipur, Chandigarh, Indore, and more.

Common questions

What is gigsnshows?

gigsnshows is a free guide to live music, stand-up comedy, theatre and sports events across India. It gathers listings from BookMyShow, District, Skillbox, thumpN, NCPA, Prithvi Theatre, The Piano Man and NMACC into one searchable place, in 65 cities.

Does gigsnshows sell tickets?

No. Every listing links to the ticketing site or venue that sells the tickets. Prices shown are the "from" prices on those sites when we last checked.

How often are listings updated?

Several times a day. Past shows are removed and new announcements are added automatically; each page shows the date it was last updated.

Which cities are covered?

65 cities, with the most listings in Mumbai, Bengaluru, Delhi, Pune and Hyderabad. The full list is on the cities page.

Is it free to use?

Yes. You can browse without an account. Logging in with a mobile number lets you keep your saved shows, plans with friends and picks on every device.

How do I report a mistake or a missing show?

Use the contact page. Corrections to dates, venues and prices are made at the source, so we check the ticketing site and update the listing.

An independent project. Questions or corrections? Use the contact page.
